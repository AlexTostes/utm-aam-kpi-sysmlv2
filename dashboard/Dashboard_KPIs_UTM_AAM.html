UTM and AAM KPI Dashboard

Four system-level KPI candidates computed from synthetic data generated from the SysML v2 focused models (UTM_Selected, AAM_Selected). Means of 30 replications per condition with 95% confidence intervals.

Synthetic scenarios with illustrative assumptions. Values describe the model's behavior, not real UTM or AAM operations.

Sources. results/summary.csv (all charts except the what-if panel) and the base-case logs UTM_OperationIntent_base.csv, UTM_OperationRecord_base.csv, AAM_TripRequest_base.csv, AAM_FlightRecord_base.csv (what-if panel, one run, seed 20261106), from the article-2 synthetic-data package.
Definitions. Capacity: accepted (UTM) or confirmed (AAM) valid demand within the shift window Δ over valid demand. Predictability: completed operations whose actual start (UTM) or arrival (AAM) is within τ of the planned or scheduled time. Calculability classes from experiment E1: C1 every input is defined in the FAA ConOps; C2 the denominator is not shared.
